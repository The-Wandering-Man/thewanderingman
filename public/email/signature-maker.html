Signature maker

Fill in someone's details, then copy their signature into Zoho. Everyone starts as Committee Member - change the role to whatever suits you.

  1. One template for everyone: Admin Console, Organization, Signature Template, Configure template. Paste the HTML code, then swap each {{NAME}}, {{ROLE}}, {{PHONE}}, {{EMAIL}} for the matching placeholder from Zoho's placeholder menu.
  2. Or per person: fill in their details above, click Copy signature, and paste it into their signature in Zoho.
  3. If a line is blank for someone (e.g. no mobile), delete that bit in Zoho so it doesn't show an empty "M".
